Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as passing Core Web Vitals?

Passing requires a 75th-percentile Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) of 2.5 seconds or less, Interaction to Next Paint (INP) of 200 milliseconds or less, and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) of 0.1 or less on mobile devices in Google's CrUX field dataset.

I already migrated to Hyvä and still fail. Can you help?

Yes. Hyvä provides a clean baseline, but heavy third-party tag managers, tracking pixels, and unreserved banner layouts still trigger LCP and CLS failures. We resolve those specific bottlenecks.

How much of my engineering team does this take?

Minimal time. We work in an isolated git branch and provide an audited pull request. Your team only conducts standard QA and deployment.
